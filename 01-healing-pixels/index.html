Session 01

I Taught Pixels to Heal Themselves

Run of show

  1. Start empty. Plant one cell (S). It grows into a creature.
  2. “No pixel knows the whole picture. Each one only sees its 8 neighbours.”
  3. Slice it in half (X) — it regrows. Let a volunteer do the cutting.
  4. Show hidden channels (H): the cells' secret signals, like chemical gradients in an embryo.
  5. Punchline: there is no blueprint anywhere. Just one tiny rule, copied into every cell.

One-liners

  • A Neural Cellular Automaton: every pixel runs the same tiny neural network (~8,000 numbers).
  • Each pixel holds 16 numbers: 4 you see (red, green, blue, alive) + 12 hidden “chemicals”.
  • Every step it looks at its neighbours, then nudges its own 16 numbers.
  • Training: we wounded it thousands of times and rewarded it for looking like the target again.
  • Biology: salamanders regrow limbs; planarian worms regrow a whole body from a fragment.
  • Based on “Growing Neural Cellular Automata” (Mordvintsev et al., Distill 2020).
