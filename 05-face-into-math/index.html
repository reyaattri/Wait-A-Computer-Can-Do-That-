Session 05

I Turned My Face Into a Math Problem

Run of show

  1. Selfie (C) — or a volunteer's. Zoom + drag so the face fills the frame. Dots appear where the photo is dark.
  2. “Can a pen visit every dot once, without lifting, by the shortest route?”
  3. Space: the first try is greedy — always go to the nearest dot. It works… then makes long ugly jumps.
  4. Watch it untangle: crossings disappear, the line gets shorter, the face sharpens.
  5. Toggle dots off (D). One unbroken line. Save the PNG for the post.

One-liners

  • This is the Travelling Salesperson Problem: shortest route visiting every city once.
  • 4,000 dots → more possible routes than atoms in the universe. Nobody checks them all.
  • Instead: a good-enough guess, then fix it. 2-opt: if two pieces of line cross, swap their ends — uncrossing always makes it shorter.
  • Dots are placed by weighted Voronoi stippling: each dot slides to the “centre of darkness” of its own patch.
  • Same maths routes delivery vans, drills circuit boards, and sequences DNA.
