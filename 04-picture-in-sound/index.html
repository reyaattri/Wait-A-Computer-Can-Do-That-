Session 04

I Hid a Picture Inside a Sound

Run of show

  1. Veil on → Space. “Anything in this sound?”
  2. R to reveal. Let it scroll in.
  3. Explain: rows = frequencies, columns = time, brightness = loudness.
  4. Type a volunteer's name → play again.
  5. Phone plays the .wav → M mic → picture survives the air.
  6. Lower “Sine waves” to show resolution vs. time trade-off.

One-liners

  • Encode: sound = Σ brightness × sin(2π·f·t)
  • Decode: chop audio into 40 ms slices, FFT each, stack them.
  • Real example: Aphex Twin's face in “Equation” (1999).
