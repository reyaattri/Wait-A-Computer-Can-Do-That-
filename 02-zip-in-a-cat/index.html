Why it works

  
    
    
  • Files are just bytes. The extension is only a hint.
    • 
    
  • A JPEG starts with FF D8 and ends at FF D9. Viewers stop reading there.
    • 
    
  • A ZIP is read from the end: its table of contents (PK 05 06) sits at the very end and points backwards.
    • 
    
  • Two readers, opposite ends, never collide. That's a polyglot file.
    • 
    
  • Real use: game mods, some malware (why scanners check contents, not names).
    •
